ASTRA OMNI PRIVACY ASSURANCE

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 19, 2026

At ASTRA OMNI AI, we believe privacy is a fundamental human right. Our architecture is built on a zero-knowledge, client-side encryption model. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when using the ASTRA OMNI AI web platform or mobile applications.

1. Information We Collect (Zero Remote Data Storage)

ASTRA OMNI does not operate central database servers to collect, harvest, or sell personal identifiable information (PII).

  • Conversation History: All chat transcripts, generated artifacts, and model parameters are stored strictly on your local device (browser LocalStorage / IndexedDB).
  • Cryptographic Vault: If you use encryption features, your data is locked using standard 256-bit AES-GCM encryption with your passphrase. We do not store or have access to your passphrases or decrypted keys.
  • API Keys: Any custom user-provided API credentials remain in your local device vault and are transmitted directly and securely to AI provider endpoints (Google Cloud / OpenAI) via TLS 1.3 HTTPS.

2. Camera and Microphone Permissions

The mobile app and web application request access to your device camera and microphone solely for the Project Astra Live AR Viewfinder and voice interaction modes:

  • Camera streams are processed strictly in real-time for spatial object perception and HUD overlays. Video feeds are never recorded, stored, or uploaded to any secondary server.
  • Audio streams are utilized solely for continuous duplex conversational voice transcription and synthesis.

3. Authentication & Anonymous Profiles

Users may choose to operate as Guest Explorer without providing any identification. When signing in using 1-click Google Sign-In or 6-digit OTP verification, session tokens are saved solely inside your local browser storage.

4. User Rights & Permanent Data Deletion

You maintain complete ownership of your data. You can exercise your right to erasure at any moment:

  • Custom Profile Deletion: Clicking "Delete Profile & Data" in the profile settings immediately shreds all local profile records, destroys session tokens, and resets your application state.
  • Shred All Sessions: Clicking "Shred All" cryptographically overwrites and wipes all conversation records from your device.
